Dear Editor,
Thank you for the opportunity to resubmit. The revised manuscript is a new study. It re-tests Meta-
Prompted Instruction Refinement (MPIR), a rubric-guided layer that refines the output of automatic
prompt optimization, after addressing each reviewer concern with new experiments and with a reframing
of the paper as a negative-result report.
What changed. We repaired the refinement loop, whose rounds were not independent attempts, logged
the outcome of every round, and ran 45 base prompts (three optimizers, nine Big-Bench Hard tasks,
up to three seeds) under decision rules fixed in a time-stamped protocol before the new experiments.
We added a serving-noise measurement with a minimum detectable effect, strict and lenient answer
extraction, oracle scoring of all candidates, a gated rubric-free control, format-protected variants, a
refiner-capability probe, a judge-validity analysis against a stronger reference judge, and a catalogue
of pipeline defects. We no longer call the pilot pre-registered; the protocol is version-controlled, not
entered in a registry, and the paper says so.
What we found. With a 1.7B model refining its own prompts the layer changed the prompt in 1 of 80 runs
and won in none of 45 cells. A much larger refiner obtained gains under strict scoring in several cells,
but most of them reverse under lenient scoring and a rubric-free rewrite reaches gains of the same kind,
so the rubric is not shown to be what helps. Two cautionary results seem useful beyond this method:
identical prompts do not score identically on a common serving stack, and a gate based on strict answer
extraction can accept rewrites that repair the answer tag while harming reasoning.
What we could not do. We could not rerun the GPT-4o and GPT-3.5-turbo configuration of the first eval-
uation, and we collected no human ratings of the rubric; the paper says so and treats the first evaluation’s
numbers as exploratory (reproduced, with the validation leak flagged, in an appendix).
For transparency: an earlier version of the first evaluation was submitted to another journal and was not
externally reviewed. It has not been published or posted as a preprint. This manuscript is not under
review elsewhere, and all authors have approved this submission.
All code, configuration files, the revision protocol with its dated amendments, per-round logs, gen-
erations and analysis outputs are available at https://github.com/millennials92/meta_
prompted_instruction_refinement.
Thank you for your consideration.
On behalf of all authors,
